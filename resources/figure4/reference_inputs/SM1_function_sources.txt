Package metadata: Homo sapiens NCBI Entrez Gene and GO associations as of 2025-09-24, GO ontology as of 2025-07-22.
FLINC gene is matched first to a unique exact NCBI symbol, then to a unique exact alias only. Ambiguous and unmapped features are retained. NCBI names, symbols, Entrez Gene IDs and links provide general annotation.
hla_gene is the HLA- prefix of the mapped NCBI symbol (HLA class I and class II are both included). The three independent GO flags test direct or inherited GOALL annotations to the predefined seeds below. Evidence-code lists are propagated to seeds and can include computational/electronic annotations. A TRUE flag means at least one seed matched, FALSE means none matched among known annotations and NA marks lack of informative GO coverage. Flags are overlapping features, not an exhaustive classification or a gene-set enrichment analysis. mhc_ii includes the class II complex, assembly, peptide antigen presentation and its regulation; ecm includes localization and matrix organization, disassembly and structural constituents; actin includes actin structure, organization, movement and binding.
